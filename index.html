SETTINGS

Phone: touch anywhere and drag, like a joystick. The further you drag, the faster you swim. Tap BOOST (bottom right) for a burst of speed; it takes a few seconds to recharge. Computer: your fish follows the mouse, or use the arrow keys or WASD. Click or press Space to boost.

The bar at the top shows what you can eat (green) and what can eat you (red). Eat to fill your meter: you grow at each mark, and a full meter clears the reef. One bite from a bigger fish and the run is over.
