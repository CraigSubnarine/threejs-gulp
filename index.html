          SETTINGS

          Phone: touch anywhere and drag, like a joystick. The further you drag, the faster you swim. Tap BOOST (bottom right) for a burst of speed; it takes a few seconds to recharge. Computer: your fish follows the mouse, or use the arrow keys or WASD. Click or press Space to boost.

          The bar at the top shows what you can eat (green) and what can eat you (red). Eat to fill your meter: you grow at each mark, and a full meter clears the level. One bite from a bigger fish and the run is over.

          Oysters on the sea floor open to show a pearl: grab it before the shell snaps shut (while you're small, the snap eats you). Every 5 pearls give a bonus skill point. Cracked (sandy) rock pillars smash if you boost into them. Bubbles drifting down hold power-ups: Shock, Shrink, Rush and Bubble Shield.

          In the Kelp Forest (behind the reef's portal), jellyfish drift about: you can never eat them, and touching one stings (you're stunned for a moment). They're on the pink chip at the end of the food chain bar.

          Each level hides its own shard in a wreck out in the wide sea (the ◆ row on the HUD shows which you've found). Find all five before the apex and they make an orb that opens a portal to another world's ocean: you start small again but keep some skills. Miss one and the world starts over, faster.

          At the end of a zone its apex predator shows up. Bite its tail (boost into it for a double bite) and stay away from its jaws. When it shakes and a red "!" appears, it's about to lunge; right after a lunge it slows down, so that's your chance.
